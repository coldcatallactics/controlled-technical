Philosophy: Overlay and Substrate

Overlays are light; substrates and their edges are dark.

Chrome has two categories; the document is the content those surfaces frame.

MaterialRoleGround
Field (light / soft)overlay, legend, paper, sheet#fbf1c7
Board (dark / medium)substrate, window interior, edge, frame#282828
Core character
  • austere, information-dense, explicit, engineered rather than decorative
  • dense over sparse, verbosity over opacity
  • flat and explicit
  • no hidden affordances
  • functional colour only
  • hairline structure

The palette is gruvbox by Pavel Pertsev (morhetz), licensed MIT/X11.
